BloqueaSitios

Blocks every site except the allowlist, and except during “class” times from a chosen Google Calendar.

Blocked sites

Every site is blocked. Use the allowlist below to let specific sites through.

* all sites — locked on
Nightly curfew: every site is blocked from 11:00pm to 6:00am, with no exceptions — the allowlist, the built-in Google services and class times are all overridden, and open tabs are closed.

Allowed sites

Sites that are always allowed, even when using * to block everything.
Supports: domain.com, *.domain.com (subdomains), domain.com/path (specific path).

Built in: Gmail, Drive, Docs, Calendar, Cloud Console and other Google services are always allowed. Magnet links are also allowed automatically; do not paste a full magnet URI below. Google Search and Images stay blocked even if you add google.com below.

Class calendar

Choose the single calendar that represents your classes. All timed events in that calendar count as class time.
